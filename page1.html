Why Choose the antidote?


The effective replacement for Porsche 996 & 997 oil filler tube (997-107-131-00-OEM)
The antidote is a replacement part designed to outperform and outlast the standard Porsche equivalent. Here are its key benefits:


Supreme Durability: The Antidote oil tube is designed to be a lifetime product, replacing the fragile stock Porsche part known by owners for its frequent breakage.

Enhanced Integrity: By eliminating the corrugated section prone to splitting, it prevents crankcase air leakage, poor idle, and dirt ingress.

Cost-Effective: Saves significant time and labor costs associated with repeated replacements of the Porsche part and its related engine diagnostic expenses.

Engine Access Ease: Its 2-part design allows quick removal of the upper tube in 1 minute, granting improved access to engine components like the intake.

Debris Catch Feature: An integral catch at the base prevents hardware like bolts from entering the engine, offering engine protection.

Condensation Prevention: The shorter tube design largely eliminates condensation buildup, a common issue in cold weather conditions.

Unhindered Maintenance: The compact design enables easy removal of the air filter, speeding up engine maintenance tasks.

Availability of Spares: In case of accidental damage, spare parts are readily available, there is no need to replace the entire unit.

Robust Oil Cap Design: Features a sturdy threaded oil cap, superior to the fragile bayonet style of the stock part.
Global Accessibility: Cost effective replacement oil caps are easily available worldwide.

Security for the track: Built-in tabs allow for wire locking of the oil cap, adding security during track use.

Comprehensive Kit: Includes all necessary new hardware for replacing the stock tube, which is not provided with the Porsche equivalent.

Premium Construction: Ultra-heavy-duty build with thermally bonded metal hardware for high strength at the tube junction.

Simplified Oil Management: Facilitates easier funnel placement and retention for oil top-ups and changes.
Affordable Upgrade: Priced only slightly higher initially than the stock Porsche tube, offering excellent value.

Does not modify any aspect of your car, your car can be swapped back to stock at any time for concours events.

If you have an aftermarket air filter system, the stock oil filler tube may not have support and be loose; this is not a problem with this product.

Customizable Caps: Compatible with a variety of aftermarket oil caps available from hundreds of vendors worldwide (read warranty terms).
